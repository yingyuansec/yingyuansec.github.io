My research interests include **Adversarial Machine Learning, AI Security, Web Security** and **HCI**, where I primarily focus on using Machine Learning/AI  for web security purposes and analysing the security of Machine Learning/AI systems. 


I was a Postdoctoral researcher at Sapienza University of Rome, Örebro University and University of Padua, where I was supervised by [Prof. Luigi Vincenzo Mancini](https://sites.google.com/di.uniroma1.it/mancini) and [Prof. Mauro Conti](https://www.math.unipd.it/~conti/), respectively. Prior to that, I was a PhD student (in Computer Science) at University of Padua (2020-2024), in the [SPRITZ Security and Privacy Research Group](https://spritz.math.unipd.it/index.html), supervised by Prof. Mauro Conti and [Prof. Giovanni Apruzzese](https://www.giovanniapruzzese.com/). I was also a visiting scholar at University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), supervised by [Prof. Gang Wang](https://gangw.cs.illinois.edu/index.html) (2023); and a visiting scholar at University of Liechtenstein (2023), supervised by Prof. Giovanni Apruzzese. The title of my PhD dissertation is "Machine learning for phishing website detection".
